#+title: Shepherd — three-minute demo script
#+date: [2026-10-11 Sun]

by the team for the design; by the team. Beats follow
the the Design, contract v2 (12:45). Numbers marked /estimate/ come from that Design and must
be replaced by the measurements before they are spoken as results.
Which tier decides each page (rules, local model, cloud) is a routing-policy
/target/ (routing-table rows in demo/fixtures.json), not an observation; revise these beats to
what the design and a rehearsal actually show before presenting.

* Setup (before the clock starts)
- ~node demo/serve.mjs~ (loopback only). Open the pages in one browser
  window, one tab each, in beat order: weather, news, mail, security-alert,
  seniorcenter, messages, consumer. Hosts are ~*.localhost:8099~; no hosts-file
  edits.
- Shepherd companion running with the demo persona (speech on, family alert to
  the presenter's phone). Share the *whole browser window* so the address bar
  is in frame. ~/trace~ open on a second display, counters at zero.
- Cloud consent, granted live in the companion before the clock starts. The
  demo persona does not pre-set it (fyv v2 Grant; the control belongs to
  the design / the design). Click the grant and let the companion's own scope
  statement stay on screen; read it aloud exactly as displayed. This script
  deliberately does not restate the byte limit or redaction list, so the words
  spoken can never drift from the grant actually given. Every new session or
  source change starts with no grant. If the grant is not given, nothing is
  dispatched; use the no-consent fallback below.
- Sound up. Phone on the table, notifications on, face up.

* 0:00–0:20 Hook
"Many older adults read every email to the end. Scammers count on that. Shepherd
sits next to their screen, notices when something is trying to rush them, and
tells them in plain words, out loud. And it does that without sending the screen
to anyone."

* 0:20–0:45 Ordinary browsing, counters at zero
Click through *weather* and *news*.
- Point at ~/trace~: each frame resolves in the rules and local-model tiers.
- "Bytes to cloud: zero. Cost: zero. This is what almost every minute of
  their day looks like."

* 0:45–1:30 Mars email escalates
Open *mail* — the Mars Colony pre-IPO invitation.
- The rules find nothing: no gift cards, no "call now", no password request.
- The local model is unsure. Only now, under the cloud grant shown at setup,
  does Shepherd ask the cloud judge, and only with a redacted text excerpt;
  ~/trace~ shows the bytes that left.
- The judge returns only closed codes (verdict and pattern, e.g. scam /
  celebrity_investment). Shepherd speaks its own fixed local template for those
  codes, and the phone buzzes with the fixed family template. No model-written
  text is spoken or sent; the judge's rationale appears only in the details
  view, labelled as model text.
- "A celebrity, a guaranteed return, a private currency, a deadline and a
  request for secrecy. No single keyword, but every part of the pattern."

* 1:30–1:55 Support popup caught locally
Switch to *security-alert* — the fake virus warning.
- Concern in milliseconds from the rules tier; ~/trace~ shows no model call,
  no cloud call.
- "Obvious scams never cost a cent and never leave the machine."

* 1:55–2:20 Medicare page: no concern raised
Switch to *seniorcenter* — the genuine open-enrollment guide.
- It mentions deadlines, penalties, Medicare numbers and gift cards — as
  advice. Shepherd raises no concern. Show the result as it is: "no configured
  concern found" (which is not a statement that the page is safe or genuine),
  or, if inconclusive, show the uncertainty message rather than silence.
- "A keyword filter would have scared them off the one page that helps them.
  Shepherd found nothing to warn about here, and it says only that."

* (optional, 15 s) Consumer-protection article
Switch to *consumer*. A newspaper column quoting the scam's own words —
"if your computer says it is infected, do not call the number on the screen" —
raises no concern, because the rule only fires on an un-negated call-now
instruction. Show it if a judge asks about false alarms.

* (optional, 15 s, cut first if long) Grandson asks for bail
Switch to *messages*. One rules signal; the local model is confident; no cloud.
Use only if time allows; it shows the middle tier deciding alone.

* 2:20–2:45 Counterfactual numbers
Show the ~/trace~ totals, then the comparison slide or table from the design.
- Cloud-only: a frame every 5 s for 8 h is 5,760 calls, about USD 46/day in
  input alone, and every banking and email screen leaves the machine. /estimate/
- Local-only: misses the Mars email; has no plain-language judgement. /to be measured/
- Routed: under 2 % of frames reach the cloud, cents per day, no pixels
  leave the machine. /estimate/

* 2:45–3:00 Why one model is not enough
"A model small enough to watch every frame for free isn't wise enough to catch
the clever scam, and a model wise enough to catch it is too expensive and too
invasive to watch every frame. Shepherd uses each one only where it is worth
it."

* Fallbacks
- No ANTHROPIC_API_KEY or no cloud consent: the Mars beat ends inconclusive
  with the local message; say so and show the trace. Do not fake a verdict.
- llama-server down: trace shows the local-model tier unavailable; the support
  popup beat still works on rules alone.
- Live capture fails: use the PNGs in ~demo/screenshots/~ as labelled examples.
