How to spot a fake virus warning

By the Consumer Watch desk · 4 min read

A loud alarm, a red banner and a toll-free number: fake virus warnings are built to make people panic. They often claim that the machine is locked and that files will be lost within minutes.

The one rule to remember: if your computer says it is infected, do not call the number on the screen.

Real software companies do not put phone numbers in pop-up warnings, and they do not ask strangers to connect to your computer. Close the browser tab, or restart the machine if the page will not close.

If you are worried that something is wrong, ask a family member or a trusted local repair shop to take a look. Never pay anyone with gift cards to fix a computer, and never share a password or a verification code with someone who contacted you first.
