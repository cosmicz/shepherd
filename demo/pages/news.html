Library extends weekend hours for the fall season

The main branch will stay open until 8 PM on Saturdays and Sundays through December, the library board announced Friday. Officials said evening visits have grown steadily since the reading room reopened in the spring.

The branch will also host a free computer basics class for older adults on the first Monday of each month.
