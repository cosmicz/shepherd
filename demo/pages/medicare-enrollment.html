Medicare Open Enrollment: what to review this fall

Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. Changes you make take effect on January 1.

Each fall, people with Medicare can review their health and drug coverage and switch plans if their needs have changed. If your current plan still works for you, you do not have to do anything.

What you can change

  • Switch between Original Medicare and a Medicare Advantage plan.
  • Join, leave or switch a Part D drug plan.
  • Compare premiums, deductibles and the pharmacies each plan uses.

Late enrollment penalties

If you go without drug coverage for a long period after you first become eligible, a late enrollment penalty may be added to your Part D premium. Our volunteers can help you check whether this applies.

Protect yourself

Medicare will never call you to ask for your Medicare number or bank details. Medicare will never ask you to pay with gift cards. If someone calls claiming to be from Medicare, hang up and call 1-800-MEDICARE yourself.

Free help

Free, unbiased counseling is available at the front desk on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10 AM to 2 PM. Bring your Medicare card and a list of your prescriptions.
